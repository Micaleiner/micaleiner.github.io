About

Born in South Africa, painting in Stuttgart since 2014.

Being a free spirit, I have always been addicted to the thrill of creating. My aim is to portray a mood or a character, or to express what I feel about the subject, and the subject of each piece decides its materials and its style.

My style is primarily impressionistic, lately moving towards surrealism. I work with brushes, palette knives, sponges and even my fingers.
