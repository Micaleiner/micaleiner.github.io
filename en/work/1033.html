NR. 1033

The Black Hole

Perhaps the way to escape the agony, pain and fear caused by the chaos in the world lies beyond the gates of space and time. The fact is: there is no escape should you find yourself at the point where light and matter can only pass inward! Tidal forces would reduce your body to strands of atoms (spaghettification), and you would eventually end up crushed at the singularity. The idea that you could pop out somewhere, perhaps on the other side, seems utterly fantastical.
