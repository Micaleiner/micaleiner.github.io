About me

I was born in South Africa and moved to Germany in 2014. Being a free spirit, I have always been addicted to the thrill of creating. Art was naturally one of my school subjects, and for the final examination my art teacher nominated me for a distinction – unfortunately I didn’t make it. I then enrolled for a bachelor’s degree in Fine Art, but after the first year I switched to fashion design and received a diploma. I started as a designer in a bridal boutique and soon owned my own fashion boutique. I have always taken giant mental leaps and started new things.

My approach to art is similar, which is why “diverse” describes my work well. The main objective is to create something that portrays a mood or a character, or expresses my feelings about the subject. It can be anything from portraits, cityscapes, wildlife and flowers to the abstract. The subject of each piece spontaneously decides its materials and style, but whatever I do must somehow make sense. Painting is therapeutic, like meditation; it is my way of finding inner peace.

My style is primarily impressionistic, though lately it has moved towards surrealism, a technique that allows the unconscious to express itself. My abstract work is usually a planned composition, rational and calm, with the excitement coming from the tension between colours and shapes. I experiment with brushes, palette knives, sponges and even my fingers, and besides oil, acrylic and watercolour I like to use structure paste, gold leaf and collage, especially in my abstract work.

I want to keep expanding the experience of painting stories and bringing emotion into my work.
